## Service photographs

All eight service photographs are genuine stock photography downloaded from Pexels on 1 November 2026. The individual photo pages identify the photographers and mark these photographs as free to use under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). That license permits free website use and modification without mandatory attribution. It prohibits implying endorsement, offensive portrayal, sale of unaltered copies, and redistribution as a competing stock service. These images illustrate services only; they do not depict CURB staff, premises or work.

Local files are deliberate fixed downloads, cropped to 1200 × 900 pixels (4:3) and JPEG-compressed for the site. No service photograph was generated with AI. Visual toning is applied consistently by the website stylesheet.

## Hero photograph and original logo

`dist/assets/hero-tools.png`: original image generated specifically for this concept using OpenAI image generation on 1 November 2026. Overhead studio photograph of professional scissors and a black clipper on a graphite worktop. Not a stock service photograph or a representation of real CURB premises.

`dist/assets/curb-mark.svg` and `curb-logo.svg`: original vector identity created for this fictional project. No reference-site branding was reused.

The original monochrome beard image (`beard-trim.jpg`, Pexels 5853391) was replaced with the genuine colour photo above. It was not artificially colourised.

`dist/assets/hero-crossed-tools.webp`: AI-edited derivative of the original hero, 2 November 2026. Built-in OpenAI image editing removed the worktop and arranged the same clipper and scissors in an X, with true alpha transparency. Prompt: One black clipper and steel scissors crossed at their middles; clipper teeth upper left, scissors tips upper right; complete tools, realistic materials, transparent background, no text or other objects. WebP encoding preserves alpha.
